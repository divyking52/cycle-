The Alps

Alpine Mountain Escape

A seven-day high-country loop linking quiet cols, glacier viewpoints and valley recovery rides across Swiss and Italian borderlands.

← All tours

Duration7 days
DifficultyHard
Distance~320 km

Route & story

You’ll roll through high pastures, timed passes and long ridge roads with daily briefings that match weather, wind and group fitness. We balance big cols with café descents and one structured recovery day mid-week.

Approximate route

Basecamp arrival · Col du Sample* · Italian lakeside · Pass traverse · Valley finish — *working names; your ops team can rename to match the live route.

What’s included

  • Certified local ride leader
  • Luggage van between stays
  • 7 nights curated lodging
  • Daily breakfasts
  • Route files & morning briefings
  • Basic mechanical support
  • Snacks / feed stops on ride days
  • Pre-trip packing & training notes

Day-by-day itinerary

Meet at base town, bike sizing, short shake-out spin and welcome dinner with route overview.
~42 km with steady climbing — introduction to pacing, support van protocol and photo stops.
~58 km / hardest elevation block. Early start option for stronger riders.
Gentle valley loop, optional café crawl or spa afternoon.
Scenic traverse into Italian scenery — longer day, group dinner.
Rolling roads and a cinematic descent into the finish town.
Flexible breakfast, luggage transfer to station/airport as arranged.
